# Canonical paint codes and 1924 window hardware schedule

Logged exact paint formulas and original window hardware specifications with Maren Lind and Caleb Okafor of Cascadia Heritage Carpentry and Paint, who keep wet-paint drawdown cards for 418 Cedar Hill Road on file.

## Exterior and interior paint codes

- Exterior cedar clapboard siding: Sherwin-Williams SW 2847 Roycroft Pewter, low-luster exterior acrylic.
- Exterior window sash: Benjamin Moore HC-154 Hale Navy, exterior satin.
- Exterior fascia, eaves, porch columns, and interior upstairs hallway trim: Benjamin Moore OC-17 White Dove, semi-gloss.
- Front porch Douglas fir tongue-and-groove decking: Sherwin-Williams Porch and Floor Enamel SW 7068 Grizzle Gray.
- Basement mechanical alcove wall: Sherwin-Williams SW 7015 Repose Gray, satin.

## Double-hung fir sash hardware

- Sash cord: Samson Spot Cord #8 cotton-braided (5/16 inch).
- Replacement sash pulleys: 2-1/4 inch mortise solid bronze from Kilian Hardware (part #P-225B).

## Parlor window weight-pocket quirk

On the left parlor double-hung window, access the cast-iron sash weight pocket from the exterior stop bead, never the interior jamb. A 1970s remodel fastened the interior stop with ring-shank nails, so prying the interior stop splits the original 1924 old-growth fir casing.
