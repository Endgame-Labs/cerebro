# EV charger assessment and discovery of active attic knob-and-tube wiring

Master electrician Priya Subramanian from Willamette Valley Electric visited to evaluate running a 50A Level 2 EV charger circuit to the detached garage. Diagnostic fee was $195 (invoice INV-2026-1008).

## Correction to 2024 inspection report

While tracing upstairs lighting loads off Circuit 14 in the north attic kneewall, Priya found 35 feet of live cloth-insulated knob-and-tube wiring still feeding the north attic pull-chain fixture and the upstairs hallway sconce. It was spliced to modern NM-B Romex inside an uncovered metal junction box buried under blown-in cellulose insulation behind the east chimney chase. This directly contradicts the 2024 Cascade Home Inspections report claiming all knob-and-tube had been removed.

## Safety quirk

Do not pack blown-in cellulose insulation back over the north attic junction box behind the east chimney chase before Circuit 14 is rewired. Burying active knob-and-tube in cellulose traps heat, creates a fire hazard, and violates Oregon residential electrical code.

## Initial quote

Priya initially quoted $2,400 to pull a 50A garage EV subpanel and remediate the Circuit 14 knob-and-tube leg together, pending a full NEC load calculation that includes the 40A rooftop solar backfeed breaker on lugs 38/40.
