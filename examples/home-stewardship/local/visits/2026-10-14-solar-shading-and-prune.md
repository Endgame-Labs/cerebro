# Panel #11 shading diagnosis and Douglas fir limb pruning

Throughout September 2026, Enphase Enlighten monitoring showed microinverter #11 (southeast corner of the south gable pitch) producing 18% less energy than neighboring panel #10 between 1:00 PM and 3:30 PM. Total September array generation was 698 kWh.

Priya Subramanian at Willamette Valley Electric ran a remote courtesy check on the Enphase IQ8M microinverter (invoice INV-2026-1014, $0) and confirmed zero fault codes, pointing instead to afternoon shadow cast by an overhanging Douglas fir limb on the southeast fence line.

We had the limb pruned on October 14, 2026. Within 48 hours, panel #11 returned to full parity with panel #10, and October output finished at 428 kWh despite shorter days.

## Rapid-shutdown disconnect location quirk

The solar rapid-shutdown AC disconnect switch is mounted on the west exterior clapboard wall behind the climbing hydrangea trellis, not beside the utility meter on the north wall. Trim the climbing hydrangea back every fall so technicians and firefighters have an unobstructed view of the red disconnect handle.
