# Boiler knocking follow-up and expansion tank correction

Tomás Herrera from Alder and Sons Plumbing returned after the Zone 2 bathroom radiator started knocking loudly during the October 19 cold snap. Invoice INV-2026-1022 was $185, net-15, with a 1-year workmanship warranty through October 22, 2027.

## Root cause corrected

Tomás confirmed his September guess was wrong: the Taco 571 zone valve on Zone 2 is not sticking and tested cleanly across ten cycles. The actual cause of the knocking was an under-charged Amtrol Extrol #30 expansion tank (which had dropped to 8 psi) combined with a weeping Spirovent Junior air scoop on the Zone 2 supply riser.

Because the upstairs bathroom radiator sits at 24 feet of static head above the basement boiler, Tomás raised the expansion tank pre-charge from 12 psi to 15 psi and tightened the brass cap on the Spirovent as a temporary hold.

## Replacement quote

Alder and Sons Plumbing quoted $680 (parts and labor, net-15 payment terms, 2-year workmanship warranty) to replace the 1-1/4 inch brass Spirovent Junior air scoop on Zone 2 and flush the basement sediment trap. We booked the replacement for November 24, 2026.
