# West kitchen window sill repair and bare-cedar primer decision

Foreman Caleb Okafor and lead painter Maren Lind from Cascadia Heritage Carpentry and Paint replaced 6 feet of rotted Douglas fir sill and two clapboard courses beneath the west kitchen window. Invoice INV-2026-1020 was $1,920, backed by a 2-year workmanship warranty through October 20, 2028.

## Primer disagreement and standing house rule

Maren initially recommended priming the bare western red cedar patches with a fast-drying 100% acrylic stain-blocking primer so both topcoats of Sherwin-Williams SW 2847 Roycroft Pewter could go on in one dry afternoon. Caleb disagreed, arguing that old-growth western red cedar bleeds tannin through acrylic within two wet Portland winters.

They ran a side-by-side test on a cut-off clapboard piece; the acrylic test showed faint amber tannin bleed after 24 hours of misting. Maren agreed with Caleb's call. The standing house rule for all bare exterior cedar repairs at 418 Cedar Hill Road is Flood Penetrol conditioning followed by Sherwin-Williams Exterior Oil-Based Wood Primer before acrylic topcoats.
