# Revised electrical quote and decision to split attic rewire from EV subpanel

Priya Subramanian from Willamette Valley Electric sent the completed NEC load calculation and revised her October 8 quote upward from $2,400 to $3,150 total. ## Disagreement and final decision

David wanted to defer the attic knob-and-tube rewire until spring 2027 to avoid a $3,150 outlay before the holidays. Nora and Priya pushed back because the uncovered junction box and live cloth-insulated conductors under the north attic cellulose should not sit through winter heating season.

On November 10, David agreed to split the work into two phases:

- Phase 1 (scheduled December 18, 2026): Remediate the 35-foot Circuit 14 knob-and-tube attic run, install a proper covered junction box, and perform a full thermal scan of the Square D QO main panel for $1,450 standalone.
- Phase 2 (deferred to April 2027): Install the 60A garage EV subpanel for $1,700.

Willamette Valley Electric's terms are 50% deposit at job start and 50% upon City of Portland BDS permit sign-off, backed by a 5-year workmanship warranty on all rewired circuits.
