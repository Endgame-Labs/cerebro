# Southwest copper downspout overflow and December reroute quote

During the November 11 cloudburst, the southwest corner copper downspout overflowed onto the poured-concrete basement foundation wall because the 1924 underground clay drain elbow is plugged with bigleaf maple roots.

Caleb Okafor inspected the downspout on November 12 and quoted $540 to disconnect the plugged clay tile, install a 3x4-inch surface copper splash extension and stainless leaf-screen diverter, and route runoff into the south gravel swale. The work is scheduled for December 8, 2026, contingent on a dry morning so butyl gutter-seam sealant cures properly.
