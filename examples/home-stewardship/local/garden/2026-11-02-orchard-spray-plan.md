# Dormant copper spray and winter pruning rules for the orchard

Planned the autumn dormant spray for the South Slope Orchard: apply Bonide Liquid Copper Fungicide to the Rainier cherry, Shiro plum, and Bosc pear after full leaf drop on November 28, 2026 to suppress peach leaf curl and bacterial canker.

## Pruning timing rule

Prune dead water sprouts from the two Honeycrisp apples and the Liberty apple in late January 2027 once temperatures hold below 45 degrees Fahrenheit for a full week. Never prune the Rainier cherry during wet winter months because winter cuts invite Pseudomonas bacterial canker in Portland's climate; prune the cherry in July or August during dry summer weather only.
