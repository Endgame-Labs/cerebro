# South Slope Orchard tree inventory, soil test, and drip-valve quirk

Updated the inventory for the six heritage fruit trees on the 1,200-square-foot south slope terrace:

- Two Honeycrisp apples on M.7 semi-dwarf rootstock (planted 2021).
- One Liberty apple, scab-resistant cultivar (planted 2022).
- One Bosc pear (planted 2020).
- One Rainier sweet cherry on Gisela 5 rootstock (planted 2023).
- One Shiro Japanese plum (planted 2022).

## Soil test and late-winter liming

OSU Extension soil lab results (sample #PDX-9941) measured terrace soil pH at 5.8, below the 6.3 to 6.5 target for apples and stone fruit. Plan to spread 25 pounds of dolomitic agricultural lime across the south slope terrace in late February 2027.

## Buried drip irrigation valve quirk

The drip irrigation Zone B shutoff valve for the South Slope Orchard sits inside a green 6-inch round valve box buried 4 inches east of the third basalt garden step under creeping thyme groundcover. Flag it before string-trimming along the stone steps so the PVC riser does not get cracked.

## Gopher protection on Bosc pear

Spotted fresh gopher mounds near the Bosc pear drip ring; David installed a 1/2-inch galvanized hardware-cloth collar around the trunk flare on September 29.
