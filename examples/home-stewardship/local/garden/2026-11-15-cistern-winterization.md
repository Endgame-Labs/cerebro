# Rainwater cistern winterization and December bib gasket repair

Completed annual winterization for the 1,500-gallon Bushman polyethylene rainwater cistern behind the north cedar privacy screen, which irrigates the six raised cedar vegetable beds and north perennial borders.

## Equipment and warranty

- Booster pump: 1/2 HP Grundfos MQ3-35 (serial GR-55102), installed May 10, 2024, 3-year manufacturer warranty through May 10, 2027.
- Storage location: David drained the cistern manifold on November 15, 2026, disconnected the Grundfos pump, and stored it indoors on shelf 2 of the basement dry bay beside the spare paint cans.

## First-flush diverter quirk

The 4-inch first-flush diverter mesh screen clogs with bigleaf maple samaras every November. If it is not pulled and rinsed before the heavy November rains, roof runoff backs up and spills under the north kitchen porch fascia.

## Upcoming repair

Found a hairline crack in the brass freeze-proof hose bib gasket on the cistern outlet manifold. Ordered a 3/4-inch EPDM replacement kit and scheduled the gasket swap for December 15, 2026 before January freezes arrive.
