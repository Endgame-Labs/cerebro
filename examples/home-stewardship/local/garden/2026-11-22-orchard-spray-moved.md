# Dormant copper spray moved from November 28 to December 10

Heavy Portland rain forecast across Thanksgiving week forced us to move the South Slope Orchard Bonide Liquid Copper Fungicide dormant spray from November 28, 2026 to December 10, 2026. The spray requires at least 24 dry hours after application so copper stays on the bark instead of washing into the terrace soil.

Walked the terrace today: the Rainier cherry has reached 100% leaf drop and shows no signs of gummosis or bark cracking this fall.
