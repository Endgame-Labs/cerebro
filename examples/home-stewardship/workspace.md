Lifetime owner's manual and maintenance wiki for 418 Cedar Hill Road, a 1924
craftsman house and half-acre garden in Portland, Oregon, stewarded by Nora
Takahashi and David Lindholm.

This wiki is shared with visiting contractors and written to hand down to
future owners. Prioritize concrete model numbers, valve and circuit numbers,
paint and hardware codes, physical quirks, warranty expirations, and dated
service history over general homeowner advice.
