Paper: Dahl et al. 2026
Date: 2026-10-28
Concepts: Sleep replay, Sleep necessity hypothesis

Neural Circuits Letters, published 2026-10-21. Dahl, Vu and Halvorsen (the same Halvorsen as the 2023 paper).

They recorded HVC in 7 adult canaries and found replay events during short daytime naps, not just at night.
Replay in naps looked like replay at night: same order, same compression. Nap replay rose in spring, the season
canaries change their song.

If replay can happen in any sleep, then the question is not whether night sleep is necessary but whether any sleep
is. That reframes the Brandt et al. 2025 result: their deprived birds could have napped by day.

Caveat: adult canaries, not juvenile zebra finches, so it may not carry over.
