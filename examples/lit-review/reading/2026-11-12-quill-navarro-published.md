Paper: Quill and Navarro 2024
Date: 2026-11-12
Concepts: Circadian gating

The preprint is now published: Journal of Avian Neuroethology, November 2026. The reading list still says
NeuroArchive preprint; the venue is now the journal.

The published version differs from the preprint I read in September. The sample is 12 birds, not 8, and the
delay in song learning is about 6 days, not 10. Reviewers made them add a group kept in constant light but given
melatonin each evening. That group was not delayed, which points at melatonin rather than at the light or at lost
sleep.

So circadian gating survives, but it is a smaller effect than the preprint claimed.
