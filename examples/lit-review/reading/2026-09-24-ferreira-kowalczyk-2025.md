Paper: Ferreira and Kowalczyk 2025
Date: 2026-09-24
Concepts: Sleep replay, Sleep necessity hypothesis

Journal of Avian Neuroethology. Ferreira and Kowalczyk played short fragments of the tutor song to 16 sleeping
juvenile zebra finches, timed to slow-wave sleep by a closed loop on the EEG. 16 controls got the same sounds at
random times in sleep.

Cued birds reached a tutor-song similarity 6 points higher at day 90 (79 against 73).

This is the first manipulation that pushes replay up rather than only measuring it, so it is the strongest
support for Sleep replay so far. It suggests sleep is doing real work, which fits the Sleep necessity hypothesis.

Open point: the paper does not say whether the people scoring similarity knew which birds were cued.

Their closed-loop rig is described in enough detail that our lab could build it. Kofi thinks four weeks.
