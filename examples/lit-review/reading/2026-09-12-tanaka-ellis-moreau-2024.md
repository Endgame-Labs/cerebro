Paper: Tanaka-Ellis and Moreau 2024
Date: 2026-09-12
Concepts: Morning song deterioration, Sleep replay

Neural Circuits Letters. 21 juvenile zebra finches recorded every day from day 40 to day 90.

Finding: song gets worse each morning before it gets better. The first songs after waking are less like the
tutor's than the last songs of the evening before. They call this morning song deterioration.

Birds with the largest morning deterioration early in learning (day 40 to day 55) ended with the best copies at
day 90. Birds with little deterioration plateaued lower.

Their reading: the night reorganises the song, which costs accuracy at first and pays off later. They link it to
replay but recorded no neurons.

Limits: behaviour only. Also they could not rule out that the morning drop is just a warm-up effect.
