The literature review for Ines Marlowe's PhD thesis, chapter 1: sleep and song learning in juvenile songbirds.
Ines is a second-year student in the Lindqvist Lab. Her advisor is Tomas Lindqvist, Kofi Mensah is the lab's
postdoc, and Priya Ramaswamy is the outside member of her committee.

The wiki is read by Ines while she drafts and revises the chapter, and by Tomas before their meetings. It keeps one
article for each paper she has read, one for each idea the review argues about, and one for each question the
papers leave open. What they need from it: what each paper actually shows and how far to trust it, which papers
support or contest each idea, and what the lab has decided.

The sources are Ines's reading notes (one file per paper read), notes from advisor and committee meetings, and the
reading list. A later note corrects an earlier one when they disagree.
