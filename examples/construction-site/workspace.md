Harrow Build is the general contractor on the Larkspur Branch Library, a four storey steel frame library at
1180 Wren Street for the Wrenfield Public Library District. Pell and Odum Architects designed it. Martin Szabo
is the owner's representative.

The wiki is read by the project manager, who runs the job from the office and needs to know, each morning,
which subcontractors are on track, which RFIs are holding up work, and what money is moving. Write for someone
who knows construction and has five minutes.
