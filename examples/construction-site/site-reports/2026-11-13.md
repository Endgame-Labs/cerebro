Date: 2026-11-13

Daily report, Larkspur Branch Library, by Luis Ferreira (superintendent).

Light snow early, cleared by 10:00. 48 workers on site.

Brightline Electric found that the fire alarm control panel shown on the electrical drawings sits on the wall where the children's reading room millwork goes. Wes Duarte issued RFI-015 on November 10.

Cobalt Mechanical needs to run hydronic piping through the level 2 shear wall at grid B-2, which the structural drawings do not allow for. That is RFI-019.

Flatness readings on the level 4 stack area came back at FF 32. The spec calls for FF 35 under the compact shelving. Keystone Concrete issued RFI-021 asking whether grinding the high spots is acceptable.

The roof is about 70 percent dried in. Martin Szabo walked the building with Dana Okafor on November 12.
