# Session 10 Recap — Midnight Watch on Pier 4

Keeping watch over Old Lantern Dock after midnight, the party caught sight of The Lantern Courier a second time. The masked courier slid a sealed warning under the tide-bell door and hurried north along the seawall with a heavy limp in the left boot, leaving a sharp scent of smoked brine-tar in the damp air.

The party recovered a broken scrap of crimson sealing wax from the packet. Under lamplight, the wax impression bore a tiny crescent crack along its left rim.

An hour later, Sera Thorne, Quartermaster of The Tidebreakers, emerged from the smuggling sluice beneath Pier 4 to parley with the party. Sera Thorne was alive and firmly in command of the dockside brotherhood. She denied stealing the Obsidian Tide-Glass and swore an oath to share Tidebreaker harbor intelligence with the party if they warned her before Salt Guild wardens swept Old Lantern Dock.
