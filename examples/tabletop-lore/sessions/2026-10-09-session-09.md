# Session 09 Recap — The Tariff Debate

The party attended an open session of the Fractured Quorum inside The Salt Exchange. Magistrate Vespera Corwin of The Salt Guild proposed raising anchorage dues from 12 gp to 28 gp per hull to fund a new harbor patrol.

Alderman Bramwell Oakhart, chair of the Free Captains' Compact, opposed the tariff hike on the chamber floor, warning that independent skippers would boycott Brineport and starve the market. The debate grew heated until Magistrate Vespera Corwin swore a formal public oath before the Quorum to honor the Free Anchorage Pact and keep Old Lantern Dock open to independent ships until the winter solstice on 2026-12-21.

Outside the marble steps, a dockside rumor began circulating that Sera Thorne, Quartermaster of The Tidebreakers, had stolen the Obsidian Tide-Glass from the old Harbor Master's vault. Several merchants repeated the accusation, though no witness had seen her near the vault.

Outside the western gate, a young watchman named Garrick sneezed twice and dropped his halberd, drawing laughs from the crowd.
