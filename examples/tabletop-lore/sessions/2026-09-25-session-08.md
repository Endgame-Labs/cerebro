# Session 08 Recap — Arrival at Old Lantern Dock

The party arrived in Brineport on a fog-bound packet boat and docked at Old Lantern Dock just after dusk. Harbor Master Captain Marlo Vance met the crew at the timber pilings and inspected their manifest by storm-lantern.

Captain Marlo Vance publicly reaffirmed the Harbor Master's Neutrality Oath, swearing before the dockhands and the party that Old Lantern Dock would keep its berths open to every vessel regardless of guild seal. Marlo wears the Brass Harbour Key on an iron chain at his belt; the key unlocks both the customs chain-gate and the tidal sluice below Pier 4.

Near midnight, the party spotted a masked figure known to the dockhands as The Lantern Courier slipping an oilskin packet under the weigh-house door. The Lantern Courier walked with a distinct limp in the left boot and vanished into the eastern pilings before anyone could hail them.

Earlier that afternoon, a drunken ferryman named Old Ned argued loudly over a sack of turnips near the slipway before wandering home.
