# Session 13 Recap — Dawn Raid and the Torn Cuff

At first light on 2026-11-20, Salt Guild wardens stormed Old Lantern Dock to enforce the Closed-Berth Writ. Using the shipping ledgers leaked by Pell Marrow, Magistrate Vespera Corwin's bailiffs arrested Alderman Bramwell Oakhart on trumped-up sedition charges and locked him in Brine Citadel. Alderman Bramwell Oakhart is now imprisoned in Brine Citadel, and his initial transfer to High Reef Quarry was set for 2026-11-29.

At the pier gate, Sera Thorne fought a brief rearguard skirmish so two Tidebreaker skiffs could clear the chain-boom. When the fog closed in, Sera Thorne vanished; the Salt Guild posted a 500 gp bounty for her capture, and across Brineport Sera Thorne is considered missing.

In the confusion behind the customs house, a guild hound cornered The Lantern Courier near the chain-winch. The courier escaped over the pilings with a pronounced left-boot limp and a strong odor of smoked brine-tar, leaving behind a torn cuff of braided harbour-blue wool identical to the Harbor Master's official dress coat worn by Captain Marlo Vance. Captain Marlo Vance remained alive at Old Lantern Dock after the raid, though guild bailiffs now watch his office day and night.
