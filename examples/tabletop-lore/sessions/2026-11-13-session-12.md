# Session 12 Recap — The Closed-Berth Writ

At an emergency midnight assembly inside The Salt Exchange, Magistrate Vespera Corwin broke her Session 09 oath to uphold the Free Anchorage Pact. Backed by guild proxies, Vespera forced through the Closed-Berth Writ on a 4-to-3 Quorum vote, revoking free anchorage at Old Lantern Dock effective 2026-11-20 and placing the pier under Salt Guild quarantine.

Alderman Bramwell Oakhart denounced Vespera's betrayal on the chamber floor and vowed to rally the Free Captains' Compact to blockade the customs house. Unbeknownst to Bramwell, his own clerk Pell Marrow had already betrayed him by selling the Compact's private shipping ledgers and cipher notes to Magistrate Vespera Corwin's agents for 400 gp.

Earlier that evening, Brother Aldric Fen met the party privately and confirmed that Captain Marlo Vance had entrusted the Obsidian Tide-Glass to him at Tidewatch Lighthouse, clearing Sera Thorne of the earlier theft rumor that had circulated since October.
