# Session 11 Recap — The Tide-Glass and the Revenue Barge

During a heavy squall, the party followed a lone skiff to Tidewatch Lighthouse on the northern cliffs. There they witnessed Captain Marlo Vance hand the Obsidian Tide-Glass to Brother Aldric Fen, Keeper of Tidewatch Priory, asking the priest to conceal the relic inside the lighthouse lantern-room before The Salt Guild could seize it.

Brother Aldric Fen accepted the Obsidian Tide-Glass and swore to hold it neutral until a legitimate Quorum charter settled the Salt Crown succession. Captain Marlo Vance returned to Old Lantern Dock before dawn without speaking to the guild wardens.

That same night, The Tidebreakers intercepted a Salt Guild revenue barge near the outer breakwater of Old Lantern Dock and seized 2,750 gp in stamped silver salt-bars. The raid lifted Tidebreakers' treasury reserves to 9,150 gp and drove The Salt Guild to hire extra harbor mercenaries, trimming the guild's liquid treasury from 48,200 gp to 44,950 gp.
