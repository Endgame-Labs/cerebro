# GM Prep — Old Lantern Dock Dossier

Old Lantern Dock is the contested timber pier on Brineport's eastern shoals. Officially administered by Harbor Master Captain Marlo Vance under municipal charter, its underside hides a tidal smuggling sluice beneath Pier 4 used by The Tidebreakers to move contraband past guild toll-boats.

Captain Marlo Vance's watch house overlooks both the chain-boom and the tide-bell door. He boots his leather gear with smoked brine-tar to resist salt rot, and an old shipboard ankle fracture gives him a slight limp in his left boot whenever the fog rolls in.
