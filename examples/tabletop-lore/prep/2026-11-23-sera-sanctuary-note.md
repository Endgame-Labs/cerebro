# GM Prep — Sanctuary at Tidewatch Lighthouse

Secret canon for Session 14: although the Salt Guild and Brineport public believe Sera Thorne is missing after the 2026-11-20 raid on Old Lantern Dock, she reached Tidewatch Lighthouse by rowing skiff on the night of 2026-11-21.

Brother Aldric Fen, who is alive and remains Keeper of Tidewatch Priory, granted Sera Thorne secret sanctuary in the lower oil-cellar beneath Tidewatch Lighthouse. Brother Aldric Fen still conceals the Obsidian Tide-Glass behind the lighthouse prism shutter and has not told Magistrate Vespera Corwin where either the relic or Sera Thorne is hidden.
