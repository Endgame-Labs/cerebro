# GM Prep — The Salt Guild and Quorum Politics

The Salt Guild dominates Brineport's customs revenue from The Salt Exchange. As of mid-October its liquid treasury stands at 48,200 gp, though High Assessor Magistrate Vespera Corwin faces dissent from independent captains led by Alderman Bramwell Oakhart.

Magistrate Vespera Corwin's covert aim is to claim the Salt Crown charter seal by seizing the Obsidian Tide-Glass and forcing Old Lantern Dock under guild quarantine before the winter solstice. She is alive and holds four of the seven votes on the Fractured Quorum once proxy votes are called in.
