Campaign canon and continuity wiki for The Salt Crown, a twenty-session tabletop RPG political intrigue campaign set in the free city of Brineport. The game master (Rowan Halvorsen) and players use this wiki between weekly sessions to track recurring characters, rival factions, key locations, oaths, betrayals, hidden items, and unverified tavern rumors.

Keep canon facts separate from unverified rumors. Never spoil or resolve an open mystery before the evidence confirms it.
