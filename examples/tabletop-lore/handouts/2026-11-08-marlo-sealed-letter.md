# Sealed Harbor Dispatch — Marlo Vance to Brother Aldric Fen

Brother Aldric,

Keep the Obsidian Tide-Glass behind the prism shutter at Tidewatch Lighthouse and breathe no word of it to the Quorum assessors. Magistrate Vespera Corwin's bailiffs have begun pacing Old Lantern Dock at shift change, and I do not trust the customs vaults to stay locked past mid-November.

I seal this letter with my iron signet ring; forgive the blurred impression, as the ring took a small crescent crack along its left rim when I wrenched the Pier 4 sluice lever last winter. If you must send word back, leave it under the tide-bell sill after dusk.

Yours in quiet watch,
Captain Marlo Vance, Harbor Master
