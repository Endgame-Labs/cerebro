# Proclamation — The Closed-Berth Writ

By majority vote of the Fractured Quorum assembled at The Salt Exchange on 2026-11-13 (four votes in favor, three opposed), the Free Anchorage Pact of October is hereby revoked ahead of the winter solstice.

Effective at dawn on 2026-11-20, Old Lantern Dock passes under Salt Guild customs quarantine. No vessel may berth without a guild manifest stamped at The Salt Exchange, and Harbor Master Captain Marlo Vance is ordered to surrender the Brass Harbour Key to Salt Guild wardens upon inspection.

Signed and sealed,
Magistrate Vespera Corwin, High Assessor of The Salt Guild
