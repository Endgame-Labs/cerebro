# The Brineport Crier — Issue 41

**QUORUM SPLITS OVER HARBOR TARIFFS.** High Assessor Magistrate Vespera Corwin pressed the Fractured Quorum on 2026-10-09 to raise anchorage dues from 12 gp to 28 gp per hull. Alderman Bramwell Oakhart of the Free Captains' Compact led independent skippers in opposition, securing Magistrate Vespera Corwin's public oath to preserve the Free Anchorage Pact through 2026-12-21.

**TAVERN WHISPERS AT THE DROWNED BELL.** Dockside gossips claim Quartermaster Sera Thorne of The Tidebreakers lifted the Obsidian Tide-Glass from the Harbor Master's vault last month, though harbor wardens have produced neither witness nor warrant.

**MARKET NOTICES.** Mina the Baker at Saltgate Market announces fresh rosemary sourdough loaves every Tuesday morning for two copper bits. Also sought: one brown mule with a notched left ear, last seen wandering behind Saltgate Market.
