# The Brineport Crier — Issue 47

**ALDERMAN OAKHART HELD IN BRINE CITADEL.** Following yesterday's dawn sweep of Old Lantern Dock, Salt Guild bailiffs arrested Alderman Bramwell Oakhart of the Free Captains' Compact on charges of smuggling and sedition. He is imprisoned inside Brine Citadel awaiting transport to the High Reef Quarry. Compact fines and seized cargo have cut the Free Captains' Compact treasury from 18,500 gp to 12,300 gp.

**BOUNTY ON TIDEBREAKER QUARTERMASTER.** Sera Thorne disappeared during the skirmish at Pier 4 and remains missing. The Salt Guild offers 500 gp for information leading to her arrest.

**HARBOR MASTER UNDER WATCH.** Captain Marlo Vance remains at his post at Old Lantern Dock under guild supervision, having delayed surrendering the Brass Harbour Key by citing an old municipal audit clause.
