Mira Solis edits the Harbor Ledger, a fictional local newsroom in Bellwether Harbor.

Preserve attribution, disagreements, and the exact scope of corrections. Treat all fixtures
as a reporting file closed on 20 November 2026, not as evidence of what happened afterward.
