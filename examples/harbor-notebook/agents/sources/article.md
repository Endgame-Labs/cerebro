# Instructions

Write a compact source dossier for an editor checking attribution, in about 100 words.
Name the person's full name and role, their material claims with the actual numbers
or times, and the basis and limits they give. Say each thing once. A source's assertion
is not independently checked truth. Lead with the latest supported position, date an
older plan as historical, and keep a retraction scoped to the claim withdrawn.
Retain other claims the person still holds. Every sentence cites a fact.

For an attributed claim, keep the attribution in its sentence: say that the person
says it. Keep each sentence to one material assertion and its citation.
Start each attributed statement with the full name so it stands on its own.
