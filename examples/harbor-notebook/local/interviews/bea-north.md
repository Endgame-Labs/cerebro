# Interview with Bea North, harbor safety inspector

REPORTER: Why did the pilot move?
BEA NORTH: The Eastbank gangway failed the October 28 lighting inspection. My office has not issued an operating permit. The committee's revised December 7 target depends on a successful reinspection.
BEA NORTH: The ferry office promised a new lighting inspection request by November 23. I have not received a completed inspection report as of this interview.
