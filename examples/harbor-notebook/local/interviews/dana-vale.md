# Interview with Dana Vale, ferry planning director

REPORTER: What supports the night ferry proposal?
DANA VALE: Our September hand count logged 1,240 boardings across five late shifts. I called those 1,240 workers at the committee, but the sheet counts boardings, not unique people. We have not deduplicated it.
REPORTER: When will service start?
DANA VALE: We seek a November 16 pilot from Eastbank to Hospital Quay, with the last return at 00:40. That is a proposal, not an operating permit.
