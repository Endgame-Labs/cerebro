# Interview with Tomas Vale, dockworkers union steward

REPORTER: Are you related to Dana Vale?
TOMAS VALE: No. I represent the dockworkers, not the ferry office.
REPORTER: What did your members find?
TOMAS VALE: Our gate log shows 860 crossings over those same five late shifts. That log covers the cargo gate only. I cannot say whether the ferry team's 1,240 includes the hospital entrance.
TOMAS VALE: A 00:40 last boat misses our 01:10 shift release. I support a trial only if workers can actually catch it.
