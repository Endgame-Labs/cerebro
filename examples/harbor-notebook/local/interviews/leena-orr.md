# Interview with Leena Orr, hospital cleaner

REPORTER: Would the proposed night ferry help you?
LEENA ORR: I clock out at 00:15 at Hospital Quay. The current 23:30 last boat leaves me paying $24 for a taxi twice a week. A 00:40 return could work for me if the gangway stays open until boarding.
LEENA ORR: I speak for myself. I have not surveyed the cleaners, and I cannot estimate total demand.
