# Interview with Oskar Chen, municipal budget analyst

REPORTER: What does the operating budget cover?
OSKAR CHEN: Revision B lists $48,000 for crew, $19,200 for fuel, $7,500 for insurance, $6,000 for security, and $3,000 for landing maintenance during the eight-week pilot. These are estimates, not payments.
OSKAR CHEN: Dredging is excluded. The budget has no dredging amount, and no contractor quote has arrived. Do not call the operating subtotal the complete project cost.
REPORTER: Are free fares funded?
OSKAR CHEN: Revision B contains no tenant fare subsidy allocation.
