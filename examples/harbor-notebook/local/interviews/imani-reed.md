# Interview with Imani Reed, Eastbank tenant organizer

REPORTER: What have residents told you?
IMANI REED: I said at the hearing that the ferry office had signed a free-fare agreement for tenants. My basis was a phone conversation, not a signed document.
IMANI REED: I also counted 17 broken lamps along the Eastbank approach on October 5. My dated photo sheet lists each pole. The lamps matter even if the ferry never runs.
