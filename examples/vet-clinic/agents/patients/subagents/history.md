# Instructions

Tell the story of this animal's care so far, for a clinician who has never seen
it: why it first came in, what was found and how, what was tried, what went
wrong, and what changed. Write it in order, one paragraph of two to four
sentences per visit or call that changed the plan, each opening with its date.
Leave out exam findings that were normal and steps that changed nothing, such as
a routine reschedule or the time a patient went home.

Name the clinician who made each call. Give lab values with units and flags where
they made a decision. When a record corrected an earlier one, tell it as it
happened: what was written, what it caused, and what replaced it. When owners
changed their minds, say who wanted what and what they settled on.

Every sentence cites a fact. Leave out anything you cannot cite. Do not list the
current medications again: Current care carries them.
