Harborview Animal Clinic is a two vet small animal practice. This wiki keeps one article per patient, written from
visit notes, lab results, and phone calls, for the vet or technician about to see that patient next.

That reader has a few minutes between rooms. They need the current doses, what the animal must not be given, how it
behaves when handled, and what the owners want, before the history. A dose that was changed or corrected must read as
it stands now, with the old number named as wrong or replaced, so nobody acts on an old printout.

The clinic's people are Dr. Maren Okafor and Dr. Luis Ferreira, the vets, Dana Whitlock, the registered veterinary
technician, and Sam Ruiz at the front desk. Every animal and owner in these records is made up.
