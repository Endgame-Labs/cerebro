# Weekly Lab Meeting Minutes — October 26, 2026

Marco Bellini started bench onboarding today, October 26, 2026, one week later than the original October 19 date due to university biosafety clearance paperwork.

## Buffer X Lot 4B Quarantine and Vendor Disagreement

Dr. Hannah Okafor immediately quarantined Buffer X — Lot 4B (BioCrest Scientific, lot_id RGT-BX-4B) and instructed Keiko Takahashi to tape a red "DO NOT USE — PRECIPITATE" warning label across the bottle and move it to the quarantine bin. Nobody may use Buffer X — Lot 4B for any Cas12a assay.

Devon Morales and Soren Lindqvist disagreed on the permanent fix. Devon argued that BioCrest Scientific's Lot 4B formulation is defective and pushed to switch permanently to Meridian Reagents for Buffer X. Soren disagreed and hypothesized that Buffer X — Lot 4B only precipitated because the 4 C cold room chilled it past its salt solubility limit; he proposed that warming Lot 4B at 37 C for 15 minutes and storing Buffer X at 20 C room temperature might resolve the precipitate without changing vendors.

Dr. Okafor ruled that we will not risk onboarding plates on a quarantined bottle: Keiko ordered Buffer X — Lot 5C from Meridian Reagents (arriving November 1) with mandatory 20 C room-temperature storage on Bench 2, replacing the old 4 C cold-room rule. Soren's warming hypothesis remains untested and unapproved for live assays.

## Reversal of Cas12a 42 C Decision

Based on Soren's October 23 side control showing faint false-positive cleavage in the 42 C no-template control, Dr. Okafor reversed the September 28 decision to incubate at 42 C. CRISPR-Cas12a Lateral Flow Assay moves to draft v2.3, returning to 37 C incubation for 30 minutes and adding a mandatory visual clarity check of Buffer X before pipetting.
