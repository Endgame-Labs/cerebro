# Failed Run Write-Up: 2x Gibson Master Mix Lot G11A Freeze-Thaw Degradation

Ran 4-Fragment Gibson Assembly v1.2 (50 C for 30 minutes) into E. coli DH5alpha-T1R using tube #2 of 2x Gibson Master Mix — Lot G11A. Tube #2 had been thawed and refrozen 7 times since September 18.

All 3 replicates from tube #2 failed: only 9, 11, and 7 colonies grew, and miniprep yields dropped to 38, 44, and 35 ng/uL. Re-running the exact same DNA fragments the same afternoon with a fresh single-use 10 uL aliquot of 2x Gibson Master Mix — Lot G11A restored full performance: 224, 231, and 218 ng/uL across 3 replicates with 89% four-fragment accuracy.

Confirmed failure mode: 2x Gibson Master Mix — Lot G11A loses exonuclease and ligase activity after 5 freeze-thaw cycles. Keiko aliquoted the remaining stock into single-use 10 uL PCR tubes at -20 C and marked tube #2 for disposal.
