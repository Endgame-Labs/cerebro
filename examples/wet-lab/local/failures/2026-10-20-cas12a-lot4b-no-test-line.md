# Failed Run Write-Up: Cas12a Lateral Flow Assay v2.2 — No Test Line (PLT-2026-1020-A)

Ran plate PLT-2026-1020-A under CRISPR-Cas12a Lateral Flow Assay v2.2 (42 C for 20 minutes) using newly opened Buffer X — Lot 4B (BioCrest Scientific, pulled straight from the 4 C cold room) and LbCas12a Enzyme Stock — Lot E20.

All 3 positive replicates failed completely: no lateral flow test line appeared on any strip, and fluorescence Ct values drifted high and erratic at 35.9, 37.1, and 38.2 (yield readout 19, 14, and 11 ng/uL). Control lines appeared normally, ruling out defective lateral flow strips.

Devon Morales hypothesized that LbCas12a Enzyme Stock — Lot E20 degraded after an extra freeze-thaw cycle or lost activity at 42 C. Planned a repeat run with a fresh unopened aliquot of Lot E20.
