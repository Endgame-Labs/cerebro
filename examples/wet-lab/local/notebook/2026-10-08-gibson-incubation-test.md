# Bench Notebook: 4-Fragment Gibson Assembly Incubation Comparison

Tested 4-Fragment Gibson Assembly using 2x Gibson Master Mix — Lot G11A (stored at -20 C, Meridian Reagents) and transformed into E. coli DH5alpha-T1R (glycerol stock Box A1, Rack 1, -80 C freezer) on LB plates with 50 ug/mL kanamycin.

Under v1.1 (50 C incubation for 15 minutes), 3 replicates yielded 86 ng/uL, 94 ng/uL, and 79 ng/uL, and colony PCR showed only 28% (5 of 18 colonies) carried all four inserts. Extending the 50 C isothermal incubation to 30 minutes raised correct four-fragment assembly to 89% (16 of 18 colonies) with clean junction sequencing and miniprep yields above 215 ng/uL.

Soren recommended updating 4-Fragment Gibson Assembly from v1.1 (15 minutes) to v1.2 (30 minutes at 50 C). E. coli DH5alpha-T1R handled standard 42 C heat shock for 45 seconds and 37 C SOC recovery for 60 minutes without slow growth on 50 ug/mL kanamycin.
