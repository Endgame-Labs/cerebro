# Bench Notebook: E. coli K-12 Delta-Lon Initial Expression Prep (v1.3)

Transformed pET28a-LbCas12a into chemically competent E. coli K-12 Delta-Lon (glycerol stock Box B3, Rack 2, -80 C freezer) following Delta-Lon Transformation and Expression v1.3. Used 42 C heat shock for 45 seconds, recovered in SOC at 37 C for 60 minutes, and plated on LB agar with 50 ug/mL kanamycin made from Kanamycin 50 mg/mL Stock — Lot K09.

Colonies grew noticeably slower than standard BL21 or E. coli DH5alpha-T1R, taking 24 hours at 37 C to reach pinpoint size. Induced expression at OD600 0.8 with 0.4 mM IPTG at 18 C overnight. Elution yields across 3 replicates were 118 ng/uL, 112 ng/uL, and 121 ng/uL, which is workable but below our 250 ng/uL target.

Soren Lindqvist hypothesized that 50 ug/mL kanamycin is slightly toxic to the protease-deficient E. coli K-12 Delta-Lon strain and that inducing earlier around OD600 0.55 might improve yield.
