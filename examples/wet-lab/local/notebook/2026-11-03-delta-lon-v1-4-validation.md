# Bench Notebook: Validation of Delta-Lon Transformation and Expression v1.4 (PLT-2026-1103-B)

Dr. Priya Nair and Marco Bellini ran plate PLT-2026-1103-B to validate Delta-Lon Transformation and Expression v1.4 on E. coli K-12 Delta-Lon with pET28a-LbCas12a.

Protocol v1.4 changes from v1.3:
- Heat shock at 42 C strictly limited to 45 seconds (never 90 seconds).
- Recovery in SOC media lowered to 30 C for 90 minutes (replacing 37 C for 60 minutes).
- Kanamycin selection concentration reduced from 50 ug/mL to 30 ug/mL using Kanamycin 50 mg/mL Stock — Lot K09.
- IPTG induction (0.4 mM) moved earlier from OD600 0.8 to OD600 0.55.

Result: 620 colonies per plate after 16 hours, and soluble elution yields jumped to 294, 312, and 285 ng/uL across 3 replicates (3/3 pass, exceeding the 250 ng/uL benchmark). This confirms Soren Lindqvist's October 6 hypothesis that 50 ug/mL kanamycin and late OD600 0.8 induction were depressing E. coli K-12 Delta-Lon yields.
