Bench protocol, reagent lot, and bacterial strain wiki for the Okafor Molecular Biology Lab at Alderbrook University. The primary reader is a new graduate student onboarding onto the bench who needs the current working protocol, active reagent lot warnings, and known failure modes before starting an experiment. Lab members include Dr. Hannah Okafor (PI), Dr. Priya Nair (Senior Postdoc and Lab Manager), Devon Morales (4th-year PhD student), Soren Lindqvist (2nd-year PhD student), Keiko Takahashi (Research Technician), and Marco Bellini (1st-year PhD student).
