Inspection debrief. Rosa went through the October 9 report (88 out of 100). Every fix is done but the walk-in, which Polar Refrigeration services Friday. Until then the short rib cools in the reach-in on the line, not the walk-in.

Labels, from today: every container gets tape with the item, the date made and the maker's initials. Anything with nuts goes in a red-lid container. Nothing nut-free ever goes in a red lid.

Hana found that the risotto is made with the house chicken stock, as the recipe card says. So the risotto has not been vegetarian since launch. Priya was upset: servers have been calling it vegetarian for a month. Decision: from Saturday, October 17, the risotto uses a new mushroom and kombu stock that Marcus will make Thursdays. Until then servers must tell guests it has chicken stock. Priya tells the floor today.

Dukkah: Dee wants the carrots made nut-free for good, with a pumpkin seed dukkah, because nut allergies come up two or three times a week. Hana said the hazelnut is the point of the dish. Rosa decided: keep the hazelnut dukkah as the standard, and keep a second dukkah of pepitas, coriander and cumin, with no nuts and no sesame, in a blue-lid container on garde manger for anyone with a nut or sesame allergy.

Halibut from Salt & Line went from $27.50 to $33.75 a pound. Marcus proposed raising the seared fish to $40 on the menu. Priya said guests already balk at $36 and the fish is our slowest main. Rosa decided to switch the seared fish to rockfish on November 1, at $32 on the menu.

Cascade raised short rib to $11.40 a pound from $9.80. Rosa raised the short rib to $36 on the menu from October 20.

Tomás was right about Saturdays: the short rib ran out at 8:40pm on October 3 and October 10. Braise days are now Monday, Wednesday and Friday.
