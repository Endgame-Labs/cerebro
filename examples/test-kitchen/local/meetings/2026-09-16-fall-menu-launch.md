Date: 2026-09-16
Meeting: Staff meeting, fall menu launch
Attendees: Rosa Delgado, Marcus Pell, Priya Shah, Dee Okafor, Tomás Reyes, Hana Kimura, Luis Ortega

Rosa walked the fall menu. It goes live Thursday, September 17, except the budino, which starts September 22 once Hana has the glasses.

Stations for fall: Tomás on grill (short rib), Dee on sauté (fish and risotto), Hana on garde manger (carrots, little gems) and pastry. Marcus runs the pass Tuesday to Saturday. Luis does morning prep and dish.

Rosa said the risotto is our vegetarian main and servers should steer vegetarians to it.

Priya asked that every server ask about allergies at the greeting, not at the order, and that the kitchen tell her the moment a dish changes an ingredient. Rosa agreed.

Prices for launch: short rib $34, halibut $36, risotto $28, carrots $16, little gems $15, budino $11.

Marcus wants the short rib braised on Mondays and Thursdays only, two days of stock at a time. Tomás thinks Thursday's batch runs out on busy Saturdays. They will watch the first two weekends.
