Note: from Dee, after the beurre blanc broke twice on Friday

The thermos was on the shelf over the flat-top, which runs it hot. It breaks above about 150F. Keep it on the rail by the pass instead, and check it with the probe every half hour: 135 to 145F. If it breaks, a spoon of cold cream and a hard whisk off the heat brings it back once. Twice, make a new one.
