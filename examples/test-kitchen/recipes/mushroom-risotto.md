Dish: Wild Mushroom Risotto
Date: 2026-09-15
Station: Sauté
Written by: Marcus Pell

Carnaroli risotto, roasted chanterelles, thyme, parmesan. Menu price $28. Our vegetarian main.

Base: par-cook the rice to 60% in batches of 2 quarts at 3pm and spread on a sheet tray. Stock: the house chicken stock from the walk-in, kept hot on the back burner.

Pickup: 6 oz par-cooked rice, stock in three additions, roasted chanterelles, finished off the heat with 1 oz butter and 1 oz parmesan. About 9 minutes.

Allergens: dairy, in the butter and parmesan.
