Dish: Charred Carrots
Date: 2026-09-14
Station: Garde manger
Written by: Hana Kimura

Charred carrots, hazelnut dukkah, labneh, hot honey. Menu price $16.

Carrots: Ridgeback Farm rainbow carrots, halved lengthwise, blanched 3 minutes, then charred on the plancha to order.

Hazelnut dukkah: toasted Ridgeback hazelnuts, white sesame, coriander, cumin, black pepper, flaky salt. Pulse coarse. One batch is 2 quarts and lasts about five days.

Labneh: Larkspur Creamery labneh, 2 oz per plate, swooshed across the plate first.

Hot honey: honey warmed with Calabrian chili, strained.

Allergens: tree nuts (hazelnut) and sesame in the dukkah, and dairy in the labneh.

Substitutions: for a dairy allergy, coconut yogurt in place of the labneh.
