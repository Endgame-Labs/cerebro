Dish: Braised Short Rib
Date: 2026-09-14
Station: Grill
Written by: Marcus Pell

Braised short rib, soft polenta, gremolata, braising jus. Menu price $34 at the fall launch.

Protein: boneless short rib from Cascade Meats, cut into 9 oz portions before searing. Season the day before with 1.5% kosher salt by weight.

Braise: sear hard in the rondeau, then onion, carrot, celery, tomato paste, 1 bottle of red wine per 10 lb of meat, and veal stock to cover. Oven at 300F for 3.5 hours, until a cake tester slides in with no pull. Cool in the liquid, uncovered, on sheet trays in the walk-in. The meat must be under 41F within 6 hours of coming out of the oven: log the time and temperature on the cooling sheet.

Polenta: coarse yellow polenta, 1 part to 4.5 parts water, finished with 3 oz butter and 2 oz grated parmesan per quart. Hold in the bain at 150F, and whisk in a splash of water every 30 minutes.

Gremolata: parsley, lemon zest, garlic, olive oil. Make it at 4pm each day, never earlier: it browns.

Pickup: reheat a portion in its jus in the salamander pan, 8 minutes. Polenta first, short rib on top, spoon jus over, gremolata last.

Allergens: dairy, in the polenta's butter and parmesan. The braise and the jus hold no dairy, no gluten and no nuts.

Substitution for a dairy allergy: polenta made in a separate pot with olive oil and no cheese. It takes 25 minutes, so the server must ring it in with the order.
