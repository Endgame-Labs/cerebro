Dish: Chocolate Budino
Date: 2026-09-20
Station: Pastry
Written by: Hana Kimura

Dark chocolate budino, olive oil, flaky salt, hazelnut shortbread. Menu price $11. It goes on the menu September 22.

Budino: 70% chocolate, whole milk, cream, egg yolks, cornstarch. Set in 6 oz glasses overnight. A batch is 24 glasses.

Hazelnut shortbread: butter, flour, sugar, ground Ridgeback hazelnuts. One finger per glass, leaned on the rim.

Allergens: egg and dairy in the budino, and wheat, tree nuts (hazelnut) and dairy in the shortbread. The budino alone holds no wheat and no nuts.

Substitution for a wheat or nut allergy: serve it without the shortbread.
