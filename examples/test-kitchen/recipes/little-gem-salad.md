Dish: Little Gem Salad
Date: 2026-09-14
Station: Garde manger
Written by: Hana Kimura

Little gem lettuce, anchovy and lemon dressing, garlic breadcrumbs, shaved pecorino. Menu price $15.

Dressing: 6 salt-packed anchovies (Salt & Line), 2 egg yolks, lemon juice, garlic, Dijon, olive oil. It is a Caesar-style dressing: guests often do not expect the fish. Hold it in a ninth pan set deep in ice, at or under 41F.

Breadcrumbs: day-old bread from the bakery, toasted with garlic and butter.

Allergens: fish (anchovy) and egg in the dressing, wheat in the breadcrumbs, dairy in the pecorino and the breadcrumbs' butter.

Substitutions: leave off the breadcrumbs for a gluten allergy. The dressing cannot be made without fish or egg: for either allergy, dress the lettuce with the lemon vinaigrette from the carrot station instead.
