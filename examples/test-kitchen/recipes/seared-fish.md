Dish: Seared Fish
Date: 2026-09-15
Station: Sauté
Written by: Dee Okafor

Pan-seared halibut, brown butter beurre blanc, crushed fingerlings, charred leeks. Menu price $36. The fish is whatever Salt & Line lands that suits a hard sear: halibut for the fall launch.

Fish: 6 oz portions, skin off for halibut. Dry on a towel-lined tray in the walk-in, uncovered, for at least an hour.

Beurre blanc: shallot and white wine reduced almost dry, then cold Larkspur butter whisked in a cube at a time, brown butter added last. Hold it in a thermos at about 140F: hotter and it breaks, under 135F it is out of hot holding. Make a new one at 7:30pm.

Pickup: fingerlings crushed with olive oil, leeks, then the fish seared 3 minutes on the first side and basted, sauce around the plate, never over the crust.

Allergens: fish, and dairy in the beurre blanc. The fingerlings are dressed with olive oil only.

Substitution for a dairy allergy: salsa verde in place of the beurre blanc.
