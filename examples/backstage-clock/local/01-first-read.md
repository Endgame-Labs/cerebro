The midnight picnic is the final scene of The Clockmaker's Picnic. Nella offers a picnic at midnight to a clockmaker who has spent his life waiting for a chime. His clock never rings. The audience waits with him, and Nella eventually eats his sandwich.
Director Ellis Vale wants the silent pause to last eight seconds. Stage manager Mara Finch worries that counting by eye will vary with the laugh. Props keeper Ivo Reed calls the hero clock Auntie. Its asset ID is P-01.
