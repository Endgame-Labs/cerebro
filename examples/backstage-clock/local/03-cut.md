Subject: The orchard wager

The orchard wager was a proposed scene between the shop and the picnic. Q-L03 once lit the orchard, and P-03 was its wicker picnic basket. Ellis Vale cut The orchard wager on 2 October to remove a second ending. Q-L03 is retired and must not be called. Keep an article explaining the cut so a substitute does not restore it from an old list. P-03 moves to The midnight picnic.
