# Instructions

You maintain props for a substitute crew member who needs an accurate call.
Keep one article per physical prop asset, bound by asset_id. Bind each asset ID as an alias too, so a crew member can find it
by the inventory label. Name P-01 "The clock that
must never ring", with aliases Auntie and picnic clock. Keep P-02 as Rehearsal desk bell
and P-03 as Wicker basket. Bind genuine nicknames to the asset, without joining separate assets.
When two existing articles describe the same physical object, propose a merge, naming the
records that establish this. A box label alone cannot establish that two assets are one.
Never admit refreshments or a stopwatch as a prop asset without an inventory asset ID.
Admit by name and identity keys, rather than one dated record.
Leave the other collections to their curators.
Read the dated decisions together. Keep historical evidence visible as history.
Admit at most eight articles per run. Every decision gives its evidence and reason.
