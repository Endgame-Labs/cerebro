Date: 2026-11-19
Author: First Mate Liam O'Connor

Weekly SOLAS fire and abandon-ship muster drill held at 15:30 UTC in calm swell. All 28 crew and science personnel mustered at boat stations in 4 minutes 12 seconds, within the 5-minute shipboard standard.

Port and starboard lifeboat davit brakes were exercised under no load and signed off by the Chief Mate. No scientific equipment or station schedules were affected by the drill.
