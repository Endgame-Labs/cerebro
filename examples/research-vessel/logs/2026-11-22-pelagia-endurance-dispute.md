Date: 2026-11-22
Instrument: AUV Glider Pelagia
Author: Celine Bouchard

Watch disagreement over AUV Glider Pelagia recovery timing after Dive 34 through a 2 Celsius cold-core eddy near Flemish Pass. Raw telemetry reported 13.10 V on the battery pack.

Celine Bouchard estimated 6 days of endurance remaining and argued to keep AUV Glider Pelagia deployed through Station NA-17: Labrador Slope with recovery on November 29 to capture the full eddy spin-down. Chief Engineer Tomasz Kowalski disagreed, warning that lithium pack voltage sags sharply in 2 Celsius water and that delaying past 48 hours risked crossing the 12.40 V ballast-pump stall limit and losing the vehicle. Dr. Maren Solberg requested a bench verification of the battery shunt calibration before reversing the recovery date.
