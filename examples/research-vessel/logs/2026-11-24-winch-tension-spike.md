Date: 2026-11-24
Station: Station NA-12: Flemish Pass
Instrument: Starboard Traction Winch
Author: Tomasz Kowalski

Deck machinery incident report at 16:05 UTC during piston coring (Core 07) at Station NA-12: Flemish Pass (depth 1,180 meters, sea state 5). A steep 4-meter cross-swell induced a snap-load while breaking the corer free of stiff clay; line tension on the Starboard Traction Winch spiked to 68.4 kN, exceeding the 60.0 kN safe working load safety limit, and hydraulic pressure hit 238 bar against the 210 bar maximum.

Deck Bosun Nadia Okafor aborted the haul immediately and recovered the corer on reduced speed. Post-haul dye-penetrant inspection by Chief Engineer Tomasz Kowalski revealed a hairline crack on the level-wind sheave pin. Chief Engineer Kowalski tagged out and grounded the Starboard Traction Winch for the remainder of Expedition SW-26-09 until a replacement forged pin is fitted in Reykjavik; no further piston or gravity coring may run on this leg.
