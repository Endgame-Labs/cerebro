Date: 2026-11-25
Instrument: AUV Glider Pelagia
Station: Station NA-14: Flemish Cap
Author: Celine Bouchard

Calibration correction for AUV Glider Pelagia battery telemetry. Bench verification against the pre-cruise test log confirmed the onboard shunt resistor scale factor was programmed 4 percent too high.

Earlier reported pack voltages of 14.80 V (November 16) and 13.10 V (November 22) and the 6-day endurance estimate were incorrect. True battery voltage after Dive 41 on November 25 is 12.65 V, only 0.25 V above the 12.40 V ballast-pump stall safety limit, confirming Chief Engineer Tomasz Kowalski's warning. Celine Bouchard reversed the November 29 plan, commanded AUV Glider Pelagia into shallow 150-meter low-power loiter near Station NA-14: Flemish Cap, and scheduled immediate early recovery for 09:00 UTC November 27 before the incoming gale. AUV Glider Pelagia will not be redeployed on Expedition SW-26-09.
