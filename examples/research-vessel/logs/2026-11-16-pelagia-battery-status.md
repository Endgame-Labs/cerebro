Date: 2026-11-16
Instrument: AUV Glider Pelagia
Author: Celine Bouchard

Iridium satellite surfacing check for AUV Glider Pelagia after Dive 21 near Orphan Knoll. Raw onboard telemetry reported battery pack voltage at 14.80 V, which Celine Bouchard estimated gives 11 days of full 1,000-meter profiling endurance remaining.

Based on that estimate, the science party planned to keep AUV Glider Pelagia in the water through Station NA-17: Labrador Slope with recovery slated for November 29. The hard ballast-pump stall safety limit for AUV Glider Pelagia is 12.40 V; below 12.40 V the glider cannot reliably pump oil to surface in cold water.
