Date: 2026-11-19
Station: Station NA-11: Orphan Knoll
Instrument: Multibeam Echosounder EM304
Author: Mateo Alvarez

Calibration correction for Multibeam Echosounder EM304. XBT cast #14 revealed a sharp Labrador Current cold-water intrusion that rendered the pre-cruise autumn sound-velocity profile obsolete.

After uploading the new XBT cast #14 sound-velocity profile, Line 19 crossover check showed depth bias dropped from 18.2 meters to 0.3 meters. The earlier 18.2-meter shoaling bias was not a transducer fault; Multibeam Echosounder EM304 is fully calibrated and ready for Flemish Cap deep bathymetry.
