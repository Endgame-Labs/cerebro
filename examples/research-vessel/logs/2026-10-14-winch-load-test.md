Date: 2026-10-14
Instrument: Starboard Traction Winch
Author: Tomasz Kowalski

Dockside load test completed for the Starboard Traction Winch prior to departure on October 29. Chief Engineer Tomasz Kowalski verified the 14 mm plasma coring cable, traction drum brakes, and hydraulic relief manifold.

Class society certification sets the Starboard Traction Winch safe working load (SWL) safety limit at 60.0 kN and maximum hydraulic circuit pressure at 210 bar. Any snap-load exceeding 60.0 kN requires an immediate halt to over-the-side coring and a dye-penetrant inspection of the level-wind sheave pin before returning the winch to service. CTD rosette casts run on the separate Aft Hydrographic Winch and are not affected by Starboard Traction Winch restrictions.
