Voyage operations and sensor wiki for chief scientists, bridge officers, and deck engineers aboard *R/V Shearwater* during Expedition SW-26-09 (30-day North Atlantic and Flemish Cap survey, October 29 to November 27, 2026).

All times and dates are UTC. Scientific instruments and deck gear carry a readiness state of `ready`, `degraded`, or `grounded`. Safe working loads, calibration tolerances, and battery cutoff thresholds are operational safety limits and must be flagged whenever telemetry or watch notes approach or exceed them.
