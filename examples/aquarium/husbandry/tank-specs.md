# Tank targets for the 2026 to 2027 season

These are the ranges keepers hold each tank to. A reading outside its range goes
on the shift note and to Rhea Castillo in life support the same hour.

Tank 1, the Kelp Forest, holds 22,000 gallons. Hold it at 12.5 to 14.0 °C,
salinity 33 to 34 ppt, ammonia under 0.05 ppm. Gus the giant sea bass, a school
of leopard sharks and the garibaldi live here. Divers go in Tuesday and Friday
mornings.

Tank 2, the Rocky Shore, holds 3,500 gallons. Hold it at 13.5 to 15.0 °C,
salinity 33 to 34 ppt, ammonia under 0.05 ppm. Sea stars, anemones, kelp crabs
and purple urchins live here.

Tank 3, the Moon Jelly kreisel, holds 400 gallons. Hold it at 18.5 to 20.0 °C,
salinity 32 to 33.5 ppt, ammonia under 0.05 ppm. The moon jelly colony is fed
rotifers and enriched brine shrimp twice a day.

Tank 4, the Octopus tank, holds 1,800 gallons. Hold it at 9.5 to 11.5 °C,
salinity 33 to 34 ppt, ammonia under 0.05 ppm. Marisol, our giant Pacific
octopus, lives here alone. Chiller CH-4 is its only chiller, with no backup.

Tank 5, the Tropical Lagoon, holds 40,000 gallons. Hold it at 25.0 to 26.5 °C,
salinity 33.5 to 35 ppt, ammonia under 0.05 ppm. Biscuit the green sea turtle
lives here with the reef fish.

Tank 6 is quarantine, behind the scenes, and holds 600 gallons. Hold it at
12.0 to 14.0 °C, salinity 32 to 34 ppt, ammonia under 0.10 ppm. New arrivals
spend at least 30 days here before they go on show.
