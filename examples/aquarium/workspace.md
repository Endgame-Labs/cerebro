The husbandry team of Port Ellery Aquarium, a public aquarium on the coast. Six
keepers, a staff veterinarian and a life support technician look after six
tanks, from a cold kelp forest to a warm tropical lagoon, and the animals in
them.

The wiki is read by the keeper starting a shift. They have ten minutes before
the first feeding, and they need to know what changed since they were last on,
which tank or animal needs a closer look, and which standing rules hold.

The team: Priya Raman (she), curator, who makes the calls on moves, diets and
equipment. Dana Whitlock (she), senior aquarist, Marisol's keeper. Luis
Ferreira (he), aquarist and diver for Tanks 1 and 5. Tess Morrow (she),
aquarist for the jellies and quarantine. Kenji Abe (he), night aquarist. Dr.
Ama Okafor (she), staff veterinarian. Rhea Castillo (she), life support
technician, who keeps the chillers, pumps and filters running.

Water readings come once a day per tank. Shift notes, vet notes and staff
memos are written by people, who sometimes disagree. Gift shop and facilities
memos land in the same inbox and are rarely about the animals.
